A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious? We've got answers.

Here's what you need to know before you compress.

Will my images be uploaded to a server?+

No. Compression runs in your browser and images remain on your device. The ZIP download library is bundled with the website; optional fonts may load from Google Fonts. Your images are not sent to those services.

Can I reduce a PNG and still get a PNG?+

Yes. Choose “Keep original format” or “PNG.” The palette optimizer tries to make a smaller PNG while keeping transparency. Lower quality can reduce the number of colors and may slightly change gradients or fine detail.

Will every image get smaller?+

Not always. Some images are already optimized. If a smaller file cannot be produced, imageCompresser keeps your original file when the output format allows it. Try a lower quality, resize, or choose Smart mode, which can change formats.

What kinds of images are supported?+

PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF and BMP still images are accepted. AVIF decoding and encoding depend on your browser. Animated GIF compression is not supported.
